# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Anime fans on macOS/Windows watching anime locally using players like MPV or VLC. They want their watch history and progress synced seamlessly and automatically with their AniList profile.

## Product Purpose
Automatically match local playback filenames to AniList media items, resolve episodes, track progress, and sync it to the AniList account with minimal friction and an aesthetically pleasing web dashboard.

## Brand Personality
Anime-themed, modern, sleek, high-contrast dark theme, and visually delightful. Responsive, fast, and gaming/otaku aesthetic.

## Anti-references
Boring enterprise SaaS tools, overly plain monochrome layouts, slow manual entry, and cluttered dashboards.

## Design Principles
1. Playback is King: The player and tracking status are the most important elements; highlight active playback with vivid, pulsing indicators.
2. Low Cognitive Load: Clean grid card views, smart grouping, and automatic multi-season resolution.
3. Dark-mode first: Designed for cozy, late-night watching; rich dark grays combined with vibrant anime accents (neon pink/cyan).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast levels for text readability, full support for dark theme preferences, and respect for prefers-reduced-motion media query.
